# Fonts

## Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

Designed by the Braille Institute of America with Applied Design Works for
readers with low vision: letters that are easily confused (I l 1, O 0, b d p q)
have distinct shapes.  Next is the 2024 successor of Atkinson Hyperlegible,
with more weights; Sightline uses four.
